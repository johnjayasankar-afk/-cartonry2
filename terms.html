Refunds

If it does not do what you needed, ask and we will refund it. Within 30 days of purchase, email us with your order number and we will refund in full: you do not have to justify it. Refunds are issued through Lemon Squeezy back to your original payment method, and typically appear within 5 to 10 business days depending on your bank. Your licence is deactivated when the refund is issued.

Every paid style can be previewed at your own dimensions before you buy, and two styles export in full for free, so you can check the output quality first. We would rather you did that than bought something that does not fit your job.

Support

Support is by email, and is best-effort by a single person: expect a reply within a few business days, not within the hour. If something is wrong with the geometry, tell us the style and the dimensions you used and we will reproduce it.

What this tool is, and is not

Cartonry is a drafting aid. It computes standard box geometry from the dimensions you give it, using the allowances described on each style. It is not a substitute for your converter's or die maker's approval. Board grades, flute directions, machine tolerances and house conventions vary, and your supplier may want allowances different from our defaults, which is why the advanced panel lets you change them.

Check before you commit to tooling. Print the PDF at 100%, fold it, and test it against the real product. We are not liable for production costs, tooling costs or wasted board arising from a dieline used without that check.

Sleeve / belly band

A band that slides over something else. Open at both ends, four panels and a glue flap, and about as simple as printed packaging gets.

Open this style in the generator → Free to export — no account, no signup.

When to use it

Wrapping a tray, banding a set of products together, adding branding to a plain box, or carrying legal copy that would spoil the pack underneath. Cheap to print, cheap to change, and it lets you update artwork without re-tooling the box.

How it is put together

Four panels wrap into a rectangular tube and glue at a single flap. There are no closures at all — the product inside holds the shape.

What to watch for

The dimensions here are the cross-section the sleeve must slide over, so measure the thing it is going around, not the space inside it. If it is going over a box, use that box's outside size. A sleeve that is snug on paper is tight in reality; give it a little room.

How it assembles

  1. Score the four creases.
  2. Wrap into a tube and glue the flap to the far panel.
  3. Slide it over the product or tray. Nothing else closes.

Telescope lid

A shallow tray sized to slide over a box you already have. You give it the box; it works out the lid.

Open this style in the generator → Free to preview at your own dimensions.

When to use it

Two-piece gift and rigid-style boxes, hamper lids, and any pack where a separate lid is more elegant than a hinged one. Pair it with the half slotted container or the four-corner tray.

How it is put together

Geometrically it is a four-corner tray. What matters is the sizing: the lid must clear the outside of the base, so its internal dimensions are your box plus two board thicknesses plus a slip clearance on each side.

What to watch for

Clearance is a judgement call and it is the difference between a lid that glides and one that jams or falls off. The generator uses 0.8 mm a side by default; set it yourself in the advanced panel. Thicker or springier board wants more, and a lid that has to go on and off repeatedly wants more still.

How it assembles

  1. Score every dashed line.
  2. Fold the two side walls down from the top panel.
  3. Fold the remaining two walls down, swinging their corner tabs in behind the sides.
  4. Glue the tabs, then check the fit over the base before making the rest.

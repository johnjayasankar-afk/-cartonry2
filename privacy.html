Cartonry

Privacy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

The short version

Cartonry runs in your browser. Your box dimensions, your dielines and the files you download are produced on your own device and are never sent anywhere, because there is no server to send them to. We do not ask you to create an account and we cannot see what you design.

What we hold

Nothing about your designs. Dimensions you type, the geometry that is computed from them, and the SVG, PDF and DXF files you export exist only in your browser and in your downloads folder.

Your licence key, on your own device. If you buy a licence and activate it, the key and an activation identifier are stored in your browser's local storage so you do not have to re-enter it. You can remove it at any time with the “Release this browser” button, or by clearing site data.

Requests this site can make

  • Licence checks. When you activate a licence, and periodically afterwards, your licence key and activation identifier are sent to Lemon Squeezy to confirm the licence is valid. Nothing else is sent — not your dimensions, not your files.
  • Analytics, only if enabled. If page analytics are switched on, they are provided by Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies, uses no client-side state and does not collect personal data or fingerprint visitors. It records aggregate page views only. If analytics are off, this site makes no third-party requests at all.

Payment

Purchases are processed by Lemon Squeezy, who act as merchant of record. They collect your payment details and billing information directly — those details never reach us and we never see or store card numbers. Lemon Squeezy provide us with order and licence records so we can support and, if needed, refund your purchase. See Lemon Squeezy's own privacy notice for how they handle your data.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies. Local storage is used only for your licence, as described above.

Your rights and how to exercise them

Because we hold no design data and no account, there is usually nothing for us to retrieve or erase. Where we do hold something — the order and licence record supplied by Lemon Squeezy — you can ask us to provide a copy of it, correct it, or delete it. Deleting the licence record will deactivate the licence. Contact details are in the footer of the main page; if none is shown, the store is not yet open.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top changes with it.
